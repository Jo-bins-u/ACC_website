About the Bible Fest

Rophe is the Bible Fest initiated by the ACC Kengeri Campus, envisioned as a faith-based platform to engage the university community with the Word of God through worship, creativity, and fellowship. The word Rophe means “to bind up the broken.” That meaning shaped the first edition, held last year, which received an encouraging response from the university community and laid a strong spiritual foundation.

Building on this legacy, Rophe ’26 marks the second edition, carrying forward the theme “Love without Measure.” It brings students and faculty together for a meaningful celebration rooted in Scripture, worship, and the boundless love of Christ.

What is Association of Christian Christites?

Association of Christian Christites (ACC) is a vibrant, student-led community of Christ University, dedicated to nurturing faith and personal growth. ACC offers a supportive, inclusive space where peers can connect with like-minded individuals, explore spirituality, and discover their full potential.

Through a balance of fun, creativity, and faith-based activities, ACC empowers students to build lasting friendships, deepen their relationship with God, and become the best version of themselves. We spread the message of Jesus and help one another lead a good Christian life, striving to make a change through our activities.

ACC creates fellowship and bonding between fellow Christites. Our vision is to create a positive atmosphere on campus and glorify God. We organize Holy Mass, Leadership Training Programmes, Retreats, Fests, Feast Celebrations, intercessory prayers, Outreach programmes, and more.
